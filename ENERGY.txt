LEAKCAM rev 1: battery energy budget
====================================

Where the battery's energy goes, so battery life can be audited and improved. Every figure is marked:
  [m] measured (test named), [d] datasheet / design value, [e] estimate to replace with a measurement.
Keep this file current when a test or a change moves a figure. The audit that closes it: tests/test104.


1. The battery
--------------

  Cells          503450 LiPo, 3.7 V nominal, 1000 mAh each [d]; JB1, JB2 for a second in parallel (2000 mAh)
  Usable         ~95 % of the charge down to the rev 1 cutoff (bug-004: 3.10 V under load) [e]
  Energy         1 cell: ~3.5 Wh usable; 2 cells: ~7.0 Wh [e]
  Self-discharge LiPo ~2-3 %/month [d]: about 25 mAh/month per cell, a floor under any budget
  Charging       ~6 h from empty at ~0.2-0.3 A [m test001]; BQ24072 6.3 h safety timer [d]


2. Always on (BL616 hibernating, K230 off)
------------------------------------------

  Consumer                                  Current        Source
  ----------------------------------------  -------------  ----------------------------------------------
  BL616 in HBN level 0                       "a few uA"     [d] (DESIGN.md); to measure: test030
  U5 TPS63802 (3V3_SLEEP), quiescent          ~11 uA        [d] TPS63802 datasheet
  Leak probe divider R71/R72 1 M + 1 M        1.65 uA       [d] 3.3 V / 2 M
  AHT20 asleep                                ~0.25 uA      [d]
  BQ24072 battery drain, no input             < 1 uA        [d]
  PGOOD pull-up R23 (no USB: open drain off)  0             [d]
  Total sleep                                 ~15-20 uA     [e] -> about 11-15 mAh/month; measured by test030


3. Wakes
--------

  Wake                       How often                    Awake                       Energy per wake
  -------------------------  ---------------------------  --------------------------  ------------------------
  Humidity slice (BL616)     every 10 min (144/day)        ~85 ms, K230 off [d]        to measure (BL616 only)
  K230 report session        every 6 h (4/day), or the     boot (test021) + agent +    to measure: test102,
                             interval the K230 asks for    check + power-off           test101 (length)
  K230 leak session          on a leak (rare)              + clip upload (Wi-Fi)       to measure: test104
  BL616 USB / BLE            only on USB power             -                           from USB, not the battery

  Measured board currents (2026-10-21, USB-C 1 VBUS only, cell full; tests 015/016/021):
    rails off, BL616 awake on USB 60.5 mA | rails on, K230 in reset 86.3 | ROM in USB boot 182 | boot peak ~227
    msh idle 204-206 | DDR bench peak 270 | NAND read 225 (50 MHz), 232 (80 MHz) | NAND program/erase 235
  K230 boot, reset release -> app init: 0.88 s at NAND 80 MHz (1.00 s at 50; UFFS mounts 0.59 s): ~0.72 J, 53 uAh
  per wake [m test021]

  The K230 session is the big item: its boot (power-on to READY, estimated 1-3.5 s on 2026-10-21, not measured)
  is paid on every wake, so boot time is battery life (test021: measure, then shorten).

5. Levers (in rough order of size)
----------------------------------

  1. K230 boot time: every second of boot at a few hundred mA, 4+ times a day (test021 target).
  2. Report interval and what a report session does (agent, Wi-Fi join, upload).
  3. The K230's session current: clocks only as high as the session needs at its busiest step; rails off fast.
  4. Sleep current: the BL616's HBN level, U5's quiescent current, the divider values (bug-004 rev 2: +3.5 uA).
