LEAKCAM rev 1: battery energy budget
====================================

Where the battery's energy goes, so battery life can be audited and improved. Every figure is marked:
  [m] measured (test named), [d] datasheet / design value, [e] estimate to replace with a measurement.
Keep this file current when a test or a change moves a figure. The audit that closes it: tests/test104.


1. The battery
--------------

  Cells          503450 LiPo, 3.7 V nominal, 1000 mAh each [d]; JB1, JB2 for a second in parallel (2000 mAh)
  Usable         ~95 % of the charge down to the rev 1 cutoff (bug-004: 3.10 V under load) [e]
  Energy         1 cell: ~3.5 Wh usable; 2 cells: ~7.0 Wh [e]
  Self-discharge LiPo ~2-3 %/month [d]: about 25 mAh/month per cell, a floor under any budget
  Charging       0.89 A into the cell (0.94 A on USB with the BL616's 51 mA) [m test001]; CV taper from ~1 h
                 (BAT 4.18 V); full charge ~1-1.5 h from ~3.6 V [e]; BQ24072 6.3 h safety timer [d]
  Protection     pack PCM trips on a short and stays off until USB is plugged [m test022]; its over-discharge cut
                 is ~2.4 V (typical, not measured; the cell sat at 2.9 V without a cut [m test001]): the battery's
                 lowest voltage (bug-004, accepted); the K230's 3.10 V cutoff is the gentler stop in sessions

5. Levers (in rough order of size)
----------------------------------

  1. K230 boot time: every second of boot at a few hundred mA, 4+ times a day (test021 target).
  2. Report interval and what a report session does (agent, Wi-Fi join, upload).
  3. The K230's session current: clocks only as high as the session needs at its busiest step; rails off fast.
  4. Sleep current: the BL616's HBN level, U5's quiescent current, the divider values (bug-004 rev 2: +3.5 uA).
