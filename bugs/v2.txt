LEAKCAM rev 2: changes for the next board revision
==================================================
From the rev 1 bring-up (2026-10). Bugs with details: bugs.txt and bugs/bug-NNN/.

Assembly: no hand-soldered wires
--------------------------------
1. LED strips: flex PCB instead of wires. The four LED boards connect to each other and to the main
   board through flex PCBs (or one flex with the four LED boards on it). Soldering the white and IR
   chain wires on rev 1 took very long.
2. Batteries: use the JST 2.0 mm pitch connector the cells come with. Matching connector on JB1/JB2,
   no cut leads soldered to pads. Mark + and - next to the connector.

Debug console
-------------
3. **No debug USB at all: do not populate the CH340X and its USB-C port.** Parts out (DBG.SchDoc): U2 (CH340X),
   USBC2, D3 (its ESD array), D2 (its VBUS Schottky), R1 / R11 (its CC Rd), C5 / C6 (U2 decoupling), R2 / R10 (the
   UART0 pull-ups only U2 needed); best removed from the schematic, so nothing on the board hangs on that path.
   - The K230 console already runs through the BL616 on rev 1 (link_mux on UART1 at 4 Mbaud, the BL616's second USB
     port, tests/test039; SPL and OpenSBI print there too): one USB device (PR1) for both consoles.
   - **This is the bug-002 fix:** the confirmed back-feed path was the CH340X's D+ pull-up (powered from the switched
     3V3 whenever the K230 runs) through D3's steering diode into VUSB; with U2 and D3 gone it does not exist
     (rev 1 workaround: U2 pins 1-2-3 bridged, bugs/bug-002).
   - Lost: only the K230 boot ROM's own messages on UART0 (before SPL); keep two test pads on UART0 TX/RX for a
     3.3 V USB-serial adapter in the lab.

From the rev 1 bugs
-------------------
4. bug-001: U1 BQ24072RGTR (C140288), not the T variant: the NTC on TS works as drawn.
   Charge time: no change needed. The ~6 h / ~0.2-0.3 A on rev 1 were an unwatched night and a wrong estimate;
   measured, U1 charges at the full 0.89 A (tests/test001), ~1-1.5 h for the 1000 mAh cell, well inside the
   6.3 h timer. No sign of U1 throttling for heat.
5. bug-002 (cause confirmed 2026-10-25: the CH340X path, removed by item 3): the rule for what stays - no data line
   may lift VUSB. D5 (USB-C 1, the K230's USB) takes its VCC from VUSB the same way: give it an ESD part without a
   VCC pin, or VCC from a rail that is off when the K230 is (the K230's USB is not used in the product, but the boot
   ROM's USB mode is). Also check R63 / K230 B7, and U1's input blocking: on rev 1 U1 back-feeds VUSB from the cell
   (4.15 V into 2.1 kohm with nothing plugged, bugs/bug-002) - harmless for the start-up, but not what the BQ24072's
   reverse blocking promises.
6. bug-003: INT4 (ball F14) tied directly to the PMU's 1.8 V, or a pull-up of 4.7 k or less (not 100 k: the
   INT4 pad pulls down ~20 kOhm, rev 1 had 0.3 V and the PMU never woke). The PMU RTC is used: keep Y1 and
   C33/C34.
7. K230 BOOT1: read 0.5 V with the BL616's IO27 floating (at the 1.8 V bank's VIL). Stronger pull-down
   (R31 lower than 10 k) or IO27 driven low by the BL616 from reset (the test firmware does; the product firmware
   too since 2026-10-22, tests/test033 - it never drove IO27 before).
8. Charger input limit from a USB host (2026-10-21, tests/test001): EN2 high / EN1 low (R8, ILIM mode) sets
   1.07 A (R5 1.5 k, KILIM 1610) and turns VIN-DPM off - the BQ24072 only backs off on a sagging input with
   EN2 low. Through an ammeter (10 A range, leads, spliced cable) the input collapsed when charging started
   and D1 blinked, no charge; the same Mac port without the meter, or a wall adapter, charges. Rev 2: EN2/EN1 from the BL616 (USB500 by default, ILIM only when the
   USB-C CC level says 1.5 A or 3 A), or USB500 (EN1 high, EN2 low) fixed.
9. bug-005: J4/J5 camera connectors - reverse the pinout (pad k -> signal of pin 23-k) so a standard Pi Zero
   15-to-22 cable folded 180 degrees over the board (cameras folded back, lens out, DESIGN.md) lands camera 3V3 on
   3V3. Rev 1: 05B20L22P (bottom contact) + that cable mirrors the order (camera 3V3 on GND); rev 1 fix is a
   top-contact connector (JUSHUO AFC07-S22ECA-00, LCSC C262642) with the cameras rotated 180 degrees and longer cables. Before
   ordering: trace each conductor camera -> cable -> connector -> pad in the folded, mounted position.
10. Sleep current (tests/test031, 2026-10-25: 35 uA asleep, ~26 uA of it the board): 3V3_SLEEP from a nano-amp
   regulator (TPS63900 buck-boost or TPS7A02 LDO class) instead of U5 TPS63802 (11 uA quiescent + 5.5 uA in its
   R22/R24 feedback divider, whose low side TI caps at 100 k): about -16 uA, nearly half the sleep. BQ24072T on battery
   4.3 uA typ stays unless the charger changes too.
11. Illumination (user, 2026-10-31): two more LED drivers (4 x TPS61161 instead of 2) and twice the LEDs (16 white +
   16 IR instead of 8 + 8). Split the LED boards for easy mechanical fitting: each small board carries 1 IR LED and
   1 white LED (16 small boards instead of 4 strips of 2 + 2), joined by the flex of item 1. Check the drivers' chain
   voltage against the 38 V OVP when splitting the chains, and the current against the cell (tests/test105).
