LEAKCAM rev 2: changes for the next board revision
==================================================
From the rev 1 bring-up (2026-10). Bugs with details: bugs.txt and bugs/bug-NNN/.

Assembly: no hand-soldered wires
--------------------------------
1. LED strips: flex PCB instead of wires. The four LED boards connect to each other and to the main
   board through flex PCBs (or one flex with the four LED boards on it). Soldering the white and IR
   chain wires on rev 1 took very long.
2. Batteries: use the JST 2.0 mm pitch connector the cells come with. Matching connector on JB1/JB2,
   no cut leads soldered to pads. Mark + and - next to the connector.

Debug console
-------------
3. No separate USB-C debug port (USBC2 + CH340X U2). Route the K230 console UART through the BL616
   and out on the BL616's USB console (the same cable as PR1). One USB cable for both consoles
   instead of the current mess. This also removes the bug-002 back-feed path (CH340X D+ pull-up via
   D3 into VUSB). Needs a BL616 UART (or the existing K230 link protocol) for the K230 console.

From the rev 1 bugs
-------------------
4. bug-001: U1 BQ24072RGTR (C140288), not the T variant: the NTC on TS works as drawn.
5. bug-002: no data line may lift VUSB: ESD arrays' VCC from their own connector's VBUS (or ESD parts
   without a VCC pin); check R63 / K230 B7. With item 3 the CH340X path is gone; D5 on USBC1 stays.
6. bug-003: K230 PMU not used (software RTC, the BL616 keeps the time). Once rev 1 is proven to run
   without it:
   - remove Y1 (32.768 kHz crystal) and its load caps C33/C34: only the PMU/RTC uses that clock;
   - remove the PMU pull-ups R45 (INT4), R6 (INT0), R20 (INT1);
   - keep AVDD1P8_RTC / AVDD1P8_LDO (D12/D11) powered from 1V8: the current K230 design guide says
     the PMU supplies "must be powered if not in use";
   - CLK32K768_XIN (D15): tie to GND through a resistor, XOUT (D16) open (the guide gives no rule for
     an unused 32 kHz input; do not leave the oscillator input floating).
7. K230 BOOT1: read 0.5 V with the BL616's IO27 floating (at the 1.8 V bank's VIL). Stronger pull-down
   (R31 lower than 10 k) or IO27 driven low by the BL616 from reset (the test firmware does now).
