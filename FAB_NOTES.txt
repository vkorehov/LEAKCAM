Order remark (everything else is set by the order options):

Do not fill or cover the non-plated holes. The 0.50 mm hole at X57.566 Y2.563 is a
microphone acoustic port and must stay open through the board.

Drill-to-copper is 0.10 mm at the BGA fan-out (U3, 0.65 mm pitch). Build as drawn,
do not enlarge the plane openings.

Keep the BGA solder mask openings separate, 0.374 mm each, do not gang them.

Via covering: Epoxy filled & capped (POFV). Required: vias sit inside pads (172 in the U3 BGA
ball pads, others in SMD pads including 0201 pads on the bottom); open vias there would wick the
solder. All other vias are tented in the solder mask.
