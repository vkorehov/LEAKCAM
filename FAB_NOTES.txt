LEAKCAM - notes for PCB fabrication and assembly
Board: MIFA, 36.5 x 65.0 mm, 6 layers, 1.6 mm finished

STACKUP
  JLCPCB JLC06161H-3313, 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer copper, 0.5 oz inner copper.
  Copper layer order, top to bottom:
      MIFA.GTL  top
      MIFA.G1   inner 1   (ground plane)
      MIFA.G2   inner 2   (power pours)
      MIFA.G3   inner 3   (power pours)
      MIFA.G4   inner 4   (signal)
      MIFA.GBL  bottom
  Not ordered as controlled impedance.

BOARD OUTLINE
  MIFA.GKO is the outline: one closed rectangle, 36.5 x 65.0 mm. Nothing else is
  on that layer. Ignore any outline geometry that may appear on other layers.

DRILL
  Two files, both containing a PLATED and a NON_PLATED section:
      MIFA-RoundHoles.TXT
      MIFA-SlotHoles.TXT
  PLATED:
      0.30 mm  619 vias
      0.60 mm   10 wire pads
      0.70 mm    6 connector pins
      0.80 mm    8 routed slots (USB-C shell legs, must be plated, they are the
                 connector's ground and are soldered)
  NON-PLATED - do not plate, do not fill, do not cover:
      0.50 mm  1 hole at X33.566 Y2.563 - acoustic port of the MEMS microphone U13.
               It must stay open through the whole board. No plating, no epoxy,
               no mask or paste in the hole.
      0.60 mm  4 holes - plastic locating posts of the two USB-C connectors.
      2.60 mm  1 hole - mounting hole.

VIA COVERING
  Required: epoxy filled and capped (plated over), applied to the 0.30 mm vias only.
  The board uses via-in-pad: 137 vias sit inside the pads of the 256-ball BGA (U3)
  and 12 more inside small SMD pads. Without filled and capped vias those joints
  drain during reflow.
  The non-plated holes listed above must NOT be filled or capped.

SOLDER MASK
  BGA U3 is mask-defined: 0.374 mm openings over 0.45 mm pads, 0.28 mm mask dams
  between them. Please keep the openings as drawn, individually - do not enlarge
  them and do not gang them into one window.

KNOWN DFM ITEMS, PLEASE BUILD AS DRAWN
  Drill to copper on inner layers is 0.10 mm for the 0.30 mm vias. This is the BGA
  fan-out at 0.65 mm ball pitch; enlarging the plane openings would break the ground
  plane under the SiP. All other holes are at 0.20 mm or more (non-plated 0.20 mm,
  plated component holes 0.20 mm, mounting hole 0.30 mm).
  Minimum annular ring 0.075 mm (0.30 mm hole in a 0.45 mm pad).
  Minimum trace and clearance 0.10 mm.

SURFACE FINISH
  ENIG preferred for the 0.65 mm pitch BGA (pad flatness). HASL acceptable if ENIG
  is not available.

STENCIL
  Cut from the paste layers, both sides:
      MIFA.GTP  top paste
      MIFA.GBP  bottom paste
  0.10 mm stainless steel, frameless, apertures 1:1 as drawn - please do not scale
  or reduce them. The board carries 0201 parts (smallest aperture 0.325 x 0.270 mm)
  and the BGA apertures are 0.348 mm.

ASSEMBLY
  Double sided: 93 parts on top, 134 on the bottom.
  U3 is a 256-ball BGA, 0.65 mm pitch - X-ray inspection requested.
  Files: BOM_ASSEMBLY.csv and PickPlace_MIFA.csv (coordinates in mm, origin at the
  bottom left corner of the board, same origin as the gerbers).
  Not placed, they are soldered by hand later: S1, S2, S3 (JST PH, through hole).
  No part at all, pads only: ANT1, B1_N, B1_P, J1, J2, JB1, JB2, JT, PR1.
  ANT1 is a printed antenna - keep all layers clear there as drawn, no copper,
  no mask changes, no test points.
