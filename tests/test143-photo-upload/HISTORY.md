## Obstacles

- Every second camera grab got no frame ("VsiCamDeviceGetPathStreaming stream failed(12)"): the closed ISP library
  cannot start a stream again after stop_stream; stop + deinit then init failed "sensor open failed"; a full close and
  open per grab leaked ~1.6 kernel threads and ~240 KB kernel heap each (22 -> 54 threads, 6.5 -> 1.7 MB in 20 checks,
  then the job hung). Fixed: the streams run while the cameras are open (vicap_cap.c). The leak job had the same bug
  since the keep-open change (3c58c78): its checks alternated nocam (2026-11-12).
- A fixed 20 settle frames: the first grab after a stream start took ~40 (black frames first, then the AE ramp). Now
  the frame is kept once AE is steady (7 frames within 2 levels; AE moves in 3-frame steps), black frames never count,
  at most 60 (2026-11-12).
- The K230's second WIFI in a session made the BL616 call wifi_link_start() on the running bridge: its SDIO restore
  reset both NetHub channels, the data channel stayed down ("NETDEV_STA Device not in RUN state"), uploads failed for
  2 min. Fixed in capture.c (2026-11-12).
- Cold: media.c asked /dev/bl616 1 s after the boot (ENOENT), all tries in ms. Retries by time now (2026-11-12).
- The BL616's own check-in while the K230's bridge shared the radio and MAC (BL616 .223, K230 .222): 3-8 s
  handshakes, 2 of 7 failed (tls -0x004e); 3 failures in a row switch to the setup firmware. Fixed: the check-in waits
  for SENT, the session (and its bridge) ends after SENT, on USB the K230 back at once (2026-11-12).
- My own runs left a leak alert and media notifications on the phone (ad-hoc scripts skipped the harness's finish):
  phone_clean() at every test's finish and at every board.py script's exit (user 2026-11-12).
- The first run counted the capture before this round's (a stale "last photo"); the ID regex took a trailing comma.
