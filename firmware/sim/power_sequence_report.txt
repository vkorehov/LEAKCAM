== 1. Cold power-up, K230_PWR goes high at t = 0 ==
   0V8  10 % at   0.350 ms   90 % at   1.145 ms
   3V3  10 % at   1.640 ms   90 % at   1.740 ms
   1V8  10 % at   1.640 ms   90 % at   1.730 ms
   1V1  10 % at   2.090 ms   90 % at   2.895 ms
   RSTN crosses 1.26 V (0.7 x 1V8) at  15.430 ms
   rule "0V8 before 1V8 and 3V3 IO":        PASS  (0V8 at 90 % 495 us before the first IO rail starts)
   rule "0V8_MIPI before 1V8_MIPI":          PASS  (beads from 0V8 and 1V8, inherit the above)
   rule "1V8_RTC not later than 1V8_LDO":    PASS  (both on the 1V8 net)
   reset released after all rails at 90 %:  PASS  (12.5 ms margin, set by R30 x C23 = 10 ms)

== 2. Power-down, K230_PWR low at t = 0 (all ENs fall together: R27/R28 pull up to K230_PWR) ==
   time to 10 %, RD1/RD2 1.5 k in parallel with the load
   light off-load (1V8 50 uA, 3V3 50 uA)    0V8   0.492 ms  1V1   0.334 ms  1V8  75.598 ms  3V3  82.064 ms
   typical off-load (1V8 0.5 mA, 3V3 0.3 mA) 0V8   0.492 ms  1V1   0.334 ms  1V8  55.587 ms  3V3  73.858 ms
   -> the core (0V8) is gone in ~0.5 ms; RD1/RD2 bring 1V8 and 3V3 down in under 0.1 s whatever
      else hangs on them. The IO rails still outlive the core by that much.

== 3. Power-cycle: off for T, then on again. Does 0V8 still come up first? ==
   PASS means 1V8 < 10 % and 3V3 < 10 % at the moment 0V8 starts rising again.
   scenario                                       0.01s   0.10s   0.30s   1.00s   2.00s   5.00s
   light off-load                                 FAIL   PASS   PASS   PASS   PASS   PASS 
   typical off-load                               FAIL   PASS   PASS   PASS   PASS   PASS 
   light off-load, BL616 UART TX left high        FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL 
   light off-load, UART + 5 SDIO pins left high   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL   FAIL 

== 4. K230 "off" but BL616 pins driven high into the 10 k pull-ups to 3V3 ==
   1 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.42 V, costing 0.29 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   1 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.38 V, costing 0.29 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   2 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.75 V, costing 0.51 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   2 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.69 V, costing 0.52 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   7 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 1.67 V, costing 1.14 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   7 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 1.58 V, costing 1.20 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   -> RD2 holds the rail low (0.4 V for 2 pins), but every pin left high costs about 0.3 mA from
      3V3_SLEEP for as long as the K230 is off, and powers the "off" IO banks, cameras and CH340X
      from 0.4-1.7 V. The firmware parks those pins (analog/input, no pull) before K230_PWR goes low
      and keeps them parked until 3V3 is up.
