   0V8  10 % at   0.350 ms   90 % at   1.145 ms
   3V3  10 % at   1.640 ms   90 % at   1.740 ms
   1V8  10 % at   1.640 ms   90 % at   1.730 ms
   1V1  10 % at   2.090 ms   90 % at   2.895 ms
   RSTN crosses 1.26 V (0.7 x 1V8) at  15.430 ms
   rule "0V8 before 1V8 and 3V3 IO":        PASS  (0V8 at 90 % 495 us before the first IO rail starts)
   rule "0V8_MIPI before 1V8_MIPI":          PASS  (beads from 0V8 and 1V8, inherit the above)
   rule "1V8_RTC not later than 1V8_LDO":    PASS  (both on the 1V8 net)
   reset released after all rails at 90 %:  PASS  (12.5 ms margin, set by R30 x C23 = 10 ms)

== 2. Power-down, K230_PWR low at t = 0 (all ENs fall together: R27/R28 pull up to K230_PWR) ==
   light off-load (1V8 50 uA, 3V3 50 uA)    0V8   0.492 ms  1V1   0.334 ms  1V8 1889.962 ms  3V3 3692.886 ms
   typical (1V8 0.5 mA, 3V3 0.3 mA)         0V8   0.492 ms  1V1   0.334 ms  1V8 188.996 ms  3V3 615.481 ms
   with 1 k bleed on 1V8 and 3V3            0V8   0.492 ms  1V1   0.334 ms  1V8  51.080 ms  3V3  55.118 ms
   -> the core (0V8) is gone in ~0.5 ms while 1V8 and 3V3 linger; the IO rails outlive the core.

== 4. K230 "off" but BL616 pins driven high into the 10 k pull-ups to 3V3 ==
   1 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 2.87 V, costing 0.04 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   1 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 1.73 V, costing 0.16 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   1 pin(s) high, 1 k bleed  -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.30 V, costing 0.30 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   2 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 3.07 V, costing 0.05 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   2 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 2.27 V, costing 0.21 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   2 pin(s) high, 1 k bleed  -> 3V3 rail sits at 0.54 V, costing 0.55 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   7 pin(s) high, light load -> 3V3 rail sits at 3.23 V, costing 0.05 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   7 pin(s) high, typ load   -> 3V3 rail sits at 2.92 V, costing 0.27 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   7 pin(s) high, 1 k bleed  -> 3V3 rail sits at 1.35 V, costing 1.37 mA from 3V3_SLEEP
   -> with the UART TX idling high the "off" K230 IO banks, cameras and CH340X sit at up to 2.9 V,
      the idle budget grows by 0.04-0.3 mA per pin (more with a bleed resistor), and the next
      power-up starts with 3V3 already up. Bleed resistors cannot fix this; the firmware must park
      those pins (analog/input, no pull) before K230_PWR goes low and keep them parked until 3V3 is up.
