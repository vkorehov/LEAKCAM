LEAKCAM - PCB LAYER STACK AND GERBER ORDER
==========================================
Board:    MIFA.PcbDoc
Stackup:  JLCPCB JLC06161H-3313, 6 layer, 1.6 mm finished, FR-4
Copper:   1 oz outer (35 um), 0.5 oz inner (15.2 um)
Source:   stack.stackup (import into Altium Layer Stack Manager)


1. PHYSICAL STACK, TOP TO BOTTOM
--------------------------------
  #   Element              Material        Thickness            Dk     Df
  --  -------------------  --------------  -------------------  -----  -----
      Top solder mask      Solder Resist    1.2 mil / 0.030 mm   3.80  0.025
  L1  Top Layer            Copper 1 oz      1.378 mil / 35 um      -      -
      Dielectric 1         PP-3313          3.9134 mil / 0.0994 mm  3.95  0.022
  L2  Mid-Layer 1          Copper 0.5 oz    0.5984 mil / 15.2 um    -      -
      Dielectric 2         Core-055        21.6535 mil / 0.550 mm  4.40  0.022
  L3  Mid-Layer 2          Copper 0.5 oz    0.5984 mil / 15.2 um    -      -
      Dielectric 3         PP-2116          4.2835 mil / 0.1088 mm  4.00  0.022
  L4  Mid-Layer 3          Copper 0.5 oz    0.5984 mil / 15.2 um    -      -
      Dielectric 4         Core-055        21.6535 mil / 0.550 mm  4.40  0.022
  L5  Mid-Layer 4          Copper 0.5 oz    0.5984 mil / 15.2 um    -      -
      Dielectric 5         PP-3313          3.9134 mil / 0.0994 mm  3.95  0.022
  L6  Bottom Layer         Copper 1 oz      1.378 mil / 35 um      -      -
      Bottom solder mask   Solder Resist    1.2 mil / 0.030 mm   3.80  0.025

  Dk and Df are values for 2.4 GHz, used for the MIFA antenna simulation.
  JLCPCB's own catalogue figures are low-frequency: core 4.6, 3313 4.1,
  2116 4.16, mask 3.8. Batch spread is about +/-0.15, so sweep the antenna
  model over that range rather than trusting a single number.

  Thin dielectrics (0.1 mm):  L1-L2, L3-L4, L5-L6
  Thick cores    (0.55 mm):   L2-L3, L4-L5
  Put ground planes on the thin side of any layer that carries signals.


2. COPPER LAYER ASSIGNMENT (as routed, MIFA.PcbDoc commit 07cc720, 2026-09-14)
--------------------------
  L1  Top      POWER tracks + 93 components + short signal escapes.
               VBAT, BAT, 3V3, 3V3_SLEEP trunks run here as tracks (no pour).
  L2  Mid-1    GROUND, solid plane, no tracks. Reference for all L1 routing
               (0.0994 mm). Plane ends at y = 94.2 mm; the antenna ground
               edge above it is the L1 strip + L6 plane.
  L3  Mid-2    POWER pours: 0V8 (194 mm2), 3V3 (156), 1V1 (115), 1V8 (69),
               0V8_MIPI (38), 1V8_DDR (9), 1V8_MIPI (7). Plus 30 short signal
               segments (52 mm total: SPI_IO1/2, BOOT1, USBP_D_P, feedback
               nets) that could not escape the BGA any other way.
  L4  Mid-3    Sparse POWER: 1V8 pour (56 mm2) and 1V1 pour (27 mm2) under
               the K230D only. Otherwise empty.
  L5  Mid-4    SIGNAL. Main routing layer (550 segments, 836 mm). Also the
               3V3_SLEEP feed to the module (40/20 mil).
  L6  Bottom   GROUND plane (1937 mm2) + 134 components: all K230D decoupling
               caps, regulators U5 U6 U7 U8 U10 U14 U15, U17, PR1, J4, D1 D2
               D4, S1 S2 S3. About 270 mm of local tracks (3V3 34 mm,
               3V3_SLEEP 15 mm, CAM2 I2C, LED_CTL) cut the plane locally.

  Note: the original plan had signal on L3 and power on L4. Routing ended up
  the other way round. This is fine electrically: L5 signals reference the
  L6 ground plane through 0.1 mm, the L3 pours reference L2 ground through
  the 0.55 mm core, and the L3/L4 pours face each other across 0.1 mm.
  Do not "fix" it back; update this file if it changes again.

  Rules that follow from this:
    - No tracks on L2. On L6 only what is unavoidable under bottom-side
      parts; anything longer splits the plane.
    - Bottom-side components cut the L6 ground plane. Keep their escape
      routing on L5 and stitch ground around each part.
    - Signals on L5 must not cross a gap in the L6 plane (component pad
      fields under U3, the regulators and J4).
    - Every power rail crossing layers needs a group of vias, not one.
      A 0.3 mm via carries roughly 0.5 to 1 A. Regulator switch nodes to
      the top-side inductors are the usual single-via spots.
    - Regulator ICs sit on L6 with their inductors and input caps on L1,
      directly behind them (inductors shielded between battery and L2
      ground). Every such cap pad needs its own via pair.


3. GERBER FILES, IN PHYSICAL ORDER
----------------------------------
  Order matters: the fab stacks the inner layers in the order given below.
  G1 is the layer immediately under the top copper, G4 immediately above the
  bottom copper. Altium Mid-Layer 1..4 map to G1..G4 in that order.

  Physical   Altium layer          Gerber file                  JLCPCB name
  ---------  --------------------  ---------------------------  ---------------
  L1         Top Layer             MIFA.GTL                     Top layer
  L2         Mid-Layer 1           MIFA.G1                      Inner layer 1
  L3         Mid-Layer 2           MIFA.G2                      Inner layer 2
  L4         Mid-Layer 3           MIFA.G3                      Inner layer 3
  L5         Mid-Layer 4           MIFA.G4                      Inner layer 4
  L6         Bottom Layer          MIFA.GBL                     Bottom layer

  Non-copper (as generated 2026-09-14, Altium 26):
             Top Solder            MIFA.GTS                     Top solder mask
             Bottom Solder         MIFA.GBS                     Bottom solder mask
             Top Overlay           MIFA.GTO                     Top silkscreen
             Bottom Overlay        MIFA.GBO                     Bottom silkscreen
             Top Paste             MIFA.GTP                     Top paste (stencil)
             Bottom Paste          MIFA.GBP                     Bottom paste (stencil)
             Mechanical            MIFA.GM, MIFA.GM2            Board outline is on
                                                                one of these; confirm
                                                                in the JLCPCB viewer,
                                                                rename to .GKO if it
                                                                is not picked up
             NC Drill              MIFA-RoundHoles.TXT          Plated holes + vias
                                   MIFA-SlotHoles.TXT           USB-C 0.8 mm slots
             ODB++                 MIFA.tgz                     Not needed by the fab,
                                                                used for review

  Altium output settings:
    Gerber X2 or RS-274X, 2:4 format, inches, leading zeros omitted,
    absolute coordinates, "Embedded apertures (RS274X)".
    NC Drill: 2:4 inches, suppress leading zeros, plated and non-plated
    in separate files if the board has any non-plated holes.
    Zip all files flat, no sub-directories, and name the archive after the
    project so the fab's parser finds the outline.


4. FAB NOTES
------------
  Minimum via:      0.3 mm hole / 0.5 mm pad (standard tier).
                    Keep to ONE drill size for vias; mixed drills cost extra.
  Impedance:        Not ordered as controlled impedance. If it is ordered
                    later, state the stackup name JLC06161H-3313 so JLCPCB
                    picks the trace widths from their own model.
  Copper weight:    1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner. Inner traces carry roughly a
                    third of the current of the same width outside; a 20 mil
                    inner trace is good for about 0.4 A at a 10 C rise.
  Antenna:          Keep-out under ANT1 on ALL SIX layers, including the
                    bottom ground plane and any connector on the far side.
